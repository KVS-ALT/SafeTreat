# SafeTreat Porchlight

> Category: Editorial
> The porch light left on: warm paper, serif headlines and a single terracotta accent. For planning in daylight.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Unhurried and neighbourly, like a community newsletter. Generous whitespace, restrained chrome.
Adapted from OpenDesign's `warm-editorial` system.

## 2. Color
- **Background** `#f7f2ea` paper · **Surface** `#fffdf9` · **Surface 2** `#f0e9dd`.
- **Text** `#1c1a17` · **Muted** `#6f675f`.
- **Accent** `#c0512f` terracotta (fill) / `#a8411f` (as text). One accent element per view.
- **Status**: danger `#b42318`, warn `#b45309`, safe `#2f7d5b`, gold `#a8790f`, teal `#237a73`.
- Borders: ink at 10% / 22%. No pure black or white.

## 3. Typography
- **Fraunces** display, weight 600, tracking −0.02em. **DM Sans** body 16px / 1.6. **Geist Mono** for numbers.
- Labels: 12px DM Sans, weight 600, small caps feel via +0.04em tracking, sentence case.

## 4. Components
- Buttons: flat terracotta fill, 12px radius; secondary = 1px ink outline. Hover raises with a soft 6% ink shadow.
- Cards: surface, 1px ink-10% border, 16px radius, no resting shadow.
- Map: OpenStreetMap tiles, lightly warmed with a sepia filter.

## 5. Anti-patterns
No gradients, no radius below 8px or above 24px, no emoji in UI chrome, no dark sections inside the light page.
