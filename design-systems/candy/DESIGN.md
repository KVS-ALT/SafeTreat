# SafeTreat Candy Shop

> Category: Bold & Expressive
> A candy wrapper you can click: cream paper, thick ink outlines, hard shadows that press down like real buttons.

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Loud, friendly and tactile, aimed at families. Adapted from OpenDesign's `neobrutalism` system:
bold borders, offset shadows, raw high-contrast layout on a warm surface with a faint dot grid.

## 2. Color
- **Background** `#fff4d6` with a 16px dot grid · **Surface** `#fffdf6` · **Surface 2** `#ffeebd`.
- **Ink** `#1d1b26` for text, every border and every shadow.
- **Accent** `#ff8a1f` pumpkin fill with ink label; `#c2410c` as text.
- **Status**: danger `#e5383b`, warn `#d97706`, safe `#1f9d55`, gold `#b8860b`, teal `#0f9488`.

## 3. Typography
- **Bricolage Grotesque** display, weight 800, tracking −0.03em. **Space Grotesk** body. Geist Mono numbers.

## 4. Components
- Everything gets a 2px ink border. Cards: 4px 4px 0 ink shadow. Buttons: 3px 3px 0 shadow; hover pops to 5px,
  press drops flat (translate 3px, shadow 0).
- Radius 12/10/8. Chips are pills with ink outline; selected = accent fill.
- Map: OpenStreetMap tiles with punchier saturation, framed in ink.

## 5. Anti-patterns
No soft blurred shadows, no hairlines, no gradients, no grey text below 4.5:1 contrast.
